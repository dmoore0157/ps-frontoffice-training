Supplemental: PROMIS Interpretation

As Natalie calls out in the video, here's the PROMIS interpretation resource by Nan Rothrock, PhD from Northwestern University:

PROMIS T-Scores: Mean = 50, SD = 10. For negative domains (pain, fatigue), higher = worse. For positive domains (physical function), lower = worse. A score of 35 on Physical Function = 1.5 SD below average.

The 5A's Framework

Evidence-based behavioral counseling for every encounter

In this segment, Dr. Davis demonstrates the 5A's in action — showing how PreventScripts uses patient notifications ("Hey Reese, how are you doing with your goals this week?") to support each step of the framework.

Quick Reference

Ask

Screen and assess current behaviors in a non-judgmental way.

Assess

Evaluate readiness for change and identify barriers.

Advise

Provide clear, personalized, evidence-based recommendations.

Agree

Collaborate on setting specific, achievable goals.

Assist

Deliver resources, referrals, and PreventScripts interventions.

Key Insight: The 5A's are the core counseling framework for every PreventScripts clinical encounter. Tailor your approach based on the patient's Stage of Change.
